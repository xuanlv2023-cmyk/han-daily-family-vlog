# Giai Đoạn 4 — Khung Mega Prompts Tiểu Phẩm Gia Đình (Chuẩn Thẻ Phân Rã & Khung 10.0s)
## Skill: `han-daily-family-vlog` (Tối ưu cho Google Veo 3.1 & Muse)

## Mục đích
Soạn thảo trọn bộ Mega Prompts độc lập (**Standalone Tagged Blocks**) cho từng phân cảnh tiểu phẩm gia đình và biểu cảm em bé hài hước của kênh Hân Daily (`@_Brand_Baby_Lead`, `@_Brand_Toddler_Boy`, `@_Brand_Mother_Lead`, `@_Brand_Father_Lead`), khóa cứng **khung thời lượng 10.0 giây (`Exactly 10.0 seconds`)**, tối ưu tuyệt đối cho **Google Veo 3.1**, **Muse**, **Kling AI (1.6/2.0)**, **Luma Dream Machine**, **Hailuo Minimax**, **Wan 2.1**.

Cấu trúc thẻ phân rã giúp khóa chặt nét mặt trẻ thơ tự nhiên (má bánh bao, mắt to tròn, môi bĩu dỗi hờn), không bị lỗi biến dạng ngón tay trẻ nhỏ, không bị mặt nhựa AI và đồng bộ khẩu hình ngọng líu dễ thương.

---

### QUY TẮC CỐT LÕI: MỖI PHÂN CẢNH LÀ 1 ĐOẠN VĂN LIÊN TỤC (ONE CONTINUOUS PARAGRAPH CHUẨN 10.0S)

Để tool chạy batch copy-paste trơn tru không bị ngắt câu, **trong file xuất `.docx` và file sinh mã**, toàn bộ nội dung của một Mega Prompt phải được viết liền mạch thành **1 đoạn văn duy nhất đúng 10.0s**, nối các thẻ phân rã bằng khoảng trắng:

```text
[SPECIFICATION]: Exactly 10.0 seconds, 9:16 vertical 1080x1920, 30 FPS, authentic candid family snapshot, unretouched real human photography, zero AI plastic look, zero CGI rendering. [CAMERA LOCK & FRAMING]: Locked-off eye-level toddler medium shot, stationary tripod framing all three family members, perfectly stable. [FAMILY CHARACTER ANCHORS]: Lead Toddler: @_Brand_Baby_Lead, super cute 3-year-old Vietnamese girl, chubby rosy dumpling cheeks, expressive dark brown eyes, high topknot bun with pearl daisy hairpin, authentic soft baby skin with natural micro-pores. Supporting: @_Brand_Father_Lead (27-year-old father with round black-rimmed glasses) and @_Brand_Mother_Lead (25-year-old elegant mother with dark wavy hair). [DYNAMIC OUTFITS]: @_Brand_Baby_Lead wearing cream knitted dress and blue cardigan, parents in matching casual stylish clothes, clean 5-finger hands. [ENVIRONMENT & LIGHTING]: Positioned in @_Location_Restaurant_Dining, marble table with golden crispy chicken plate, warm restaurant ambient lights. [INITIAL STATE]: Toddler sitting between parents, mouth wide open awaiting food, eyes sparkling with innocent anticipation. [COMEDY SKIT & REACTION TIMELINE]: - [0.0s - 3.0s]: Father holds fork with crispy chicken moving toward toddler's mouth, toddler opens mouth wide blowing innocently. - [3.0s - 7.0s]: Sudden comedic twist, father turns fork directly into mother's mouth, mother bites chicken happily; toddler freezes with mouth still agape in disbelief, cheeks puffing out. - [7.0s - 10.0s]: Lower lip juts forward into iconic sulk, eyes shift into dramatic epic side-eye glare at father while parents burst into contagious laughter. [AUDIO DIALOGUE & BABY LIP-SYNC]: Toddler (ngọng líu Miền Nam): "Mẹ kì zậy... con thổi nguội gòi mà mẹ ăn hết trơn!", Father and Mother: (burst into authentic chuckles). [AUDIO BGM & COMEDY SFX]: Cartoon spring boing SFX as cheeks puff out, playful acoustic ukulele bed at -20dB, unfiltered warm parents laughter. [FINAL STATE]: Toddler maintaining hilarious dramatic pout, parents laughing warmly. [NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS]: Strictly NO extra fingers, NO fused hands, NO distorted baby limbs, NO waxy plastic doll face, NO 3D Pixar anime look, NO text overlays, NO watermarks.
```

```text
Authentic raw 8k DSLR candid photograph of a super cute 3-year-old Vietnamese girl (Bé Hân) with chubby rosy dumpling cheeks and high bun with pearl daisy pin, sitting between her father with round glasses and stylish mother at a restaurant table. The toddler has an iconic hilarious sulking face with puffed cheeks, exaggerated lower lip pouting downward, and eyes giving a dramatic epic side-eye glare at her smiling father, natural child skin pores, warm indoor lighting, 9:16 vertical ratio, shot on Sony A7R V with 85mm f/1.4 lens, ultra photorealistic, zero CGI, zero plastic skin.
```
